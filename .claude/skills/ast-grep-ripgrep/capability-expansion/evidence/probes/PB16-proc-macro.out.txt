  RECORDED   `disabled` and `server_failed` are INDISTINGUISHABLE from what
             `load_workspace_at` returns: both are `client=None`.
             The distinction IS made internally -- load-cargo-lib.rs:111-134
             maps them to ProcMacroLoadingError::Disabled and ::ProcMacroSrvError
             -- and is discarded at line 204 by `and_then(Result::ok)`.
             It survives per crate in the `proc_macros` map (lines 158-180),
             so §3.2's value is reachable, but NOT from this return type.
             An adapter must read the per-crate ProcMacroLoadResult, and a
             `project-load` family that recorded only the Option would collapse
             two states evidence 06 says must not be collapsed.
